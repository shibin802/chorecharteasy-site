Support and feedback

Contact ChoreChartEasy

Email

For questions, product feedback, accessibility issues, or privacy requests, email support@chorecharteasy.com.

ChoreChartEasy is the name used for this website. The current Service is free and does not provide account or purchase support.

What to include

  • the page or feature you were using;
  • your browser and device type;
  • what you expected and what happened;
  • a non-sensitive screenshot, if useful.

Do not send payment-card details, passwords, one-time codes, full child names, school information, health details, or chart content.

Privacy requests

Use “Privacy request” in the subject and describe the request. Most chart content stays only in your browser and can be removed with “Clear local data.” We may need to verify a request involving an email conversation.

Safety note

ChoreChartEasy provides general organizational tools. It is not an emergency, medical, psychological, legal, child-development, or safety-advice service. For an immediate concern, contact an appropriate local emergency or qualified professional service.

Response timing

We try to respond as soon as practical. We do not guarantee a specific response time. Requests governed by applicable law will be handled within the time required by that law.

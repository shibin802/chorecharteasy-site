This Cookie Policy explains how **[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]** (“ChoreChartEasy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and local browser storage on `https://chorecharteasy.com`.

## 1. What These Technologies Are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website or service provider in your browser. Local storage is a browser feature that can keep settings and chart data on your device. Similar technologies may store identifiers or help deliver, secure, and measure a service.

## 2. Necessary Storage

Necessary storage supports features you request and cannot be disabled through our analytics settings. It may include:

- your cookie-consent choice and version;
- one active chart draft and chart preferences in local storage;
- security, load-balancing, or session controls required by Cloudflare;
- authentication/session storage only if you choose a future sign-in feature.

You can remove local chart data using “Clear local data” or your browser settings. Removing necessary storage may reset your chart or preferences.

## 3. Analytics Cookies

Analytics is optional and off until you consent.

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may set identifiers such as `_ga` and `_ga_*` to distinguish browsers/sessions and measure page and feature usage. Exact durations depend on our configured settings and Google’s current documentation: **[VERIFY COOKIE DURATION BEFORE PUBLICATION]**.

We configure analytics events not to include child names, exact ages, chart titles, chore text, completion details, email addresses, account identifiers, or order identifiers.

## 4. Session Replay

Microsoft Clarity is disabled during the current validation phase. If it is enabled later:

- it will load only after analytics consent;
- this Policy and the cookie inventory will be updated;
- all chart inputs and user-generated output must be masked and verified in actual recordings;
- legal, checkout, and account pages will not be recorded;
- users will be able to withdraw consent through Cookie settings.

Do not publish a Clarity cookie list until its production configuration has been tested.

## 5. Advertising

We do not currently use advertising cookies or cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will update this Policy and request any consent required before enabling the new processing.

## 6. Your Choices

On your first visit, you can:

- `Accept analytics`;
- `Reject non-essential`;
- open `Cookie settings`.

Rejecting analytics does not block the free chart maker. You can change your choice at any time through `Cookie settings` in the footer.

Where supported, a Global Privacy Control signal keeps non-essential processing disabled.

You can also remove cookies through your browser. Browser controls may not remove chart local storage unless you clear all site data or use “Clear local data.”

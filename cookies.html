Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Policy explains how the website known as ChoreChartEasy uses cookies and local browser storage at https://chorecharteasy.com.

1. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small values stored by a website or service provider. Local storage is a browser feature that can keep settings and chart data on your device.

2. Necessary storage

Necessary storage supports features you request and is not controlled by the analytics choice. ChoreChartEasy uses local storage for one active chart draft and your consent choice. Cloudflare may also use necessary security or network controls to deliver and protect the site.

You can remove the active draft with “Clear local data” or browser controls. Removing necessary storage may reset the chart or ask for your consent choice again.

3. Optional analytics

Analytics is off until you choose “Accept analytics.” If accepted, Google Analytics 4 loads and may set first-party identifiers such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browsers and sessions.

Analytics events use an allowlist and do not include child names, exact ages, chart titles, task text, completion details, email addresses, account identifiers, or order identifiers. ChoreChartEasy does not use advertising cookies or session replay in the current Service.

5. Your choices

  • Accept analytics.
  • Reject non-essential storage.
  • Open Cookie settings from the footer to change your choice.
  • Use browser controls to clear cookies and site data.

Rejecting analytics does not block the free chart maker. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics and attempts to remove first-party Google Analytics cookies available to the site. Where supported, Global Privacy Control keeps analytics disabled.

6. Updates

We may update this Policy when storage practices or providers change. We will update the date above and request a new choice when appropriate.

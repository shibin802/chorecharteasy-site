Refund Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Current status

The preview offers Plus as a USD $1 monthly test subscription for watermark-free printing. Stripe test checkout takes no real payment, so test transactions cannot produce a monetary refund. Free printing includes a watermark.

2. Unexpected charge

If any page associated with ChoreChartEasy unexpectedly requests or appears to make a real charge, stop and email support@chorecharteasy.com. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or one-time codes.

3. Future paid products

Use Manage subscription in your account to cancel renewal. Access continues until the end of the current paid period, then prints include a watermark again. Before accepting real payments, we will publish the applicable refund terms. Contact support about billing problems; this does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

4. Contact

Questions about the current free-service status can be sent to support@chorecharteasy.com.

Create a high-fidelity DESKTOP homepage screen, 1440px wide, for ChoreChartEasy — a free printable chore chart maker for parents and caregivers of children ages 3–12.

VISUAL DIRECTION C — MODULAR FRIDGE BOARD
Build a bright, structured family utility inspired by a neatly organized refrigerator planner and quality stationery. It may feel energetic, but it must remain adult-facing, calm enough for long-form content, and clearly printable. Avoid childish cartoons and generic SaaS styling.

STYLE SYSTEM
- Background pale mint-paper #EEF4EF.
- Main surface #FFFDF8.
- Text deep teal #153B3D.
- Primary dark teal #176B65.
- CTA coral-red #DE604D.
- Accent sun yellow #F2C14E.
- Secondary blue-gray #557A83.
- Borders #B7CDC5.
- Typography: Bricolage Grotesque for headings and IBM Plex Sans for body/UI.
- Modular grid with colored section tabs, checklist markers, clipped-paper edges, solid offset borders, and nearly no shadows.
- Use varied composition: stacked tool zones and asymmetrical paper previews, not repetitive three-card rows.
- No gradients, purple, glass effects, 3D blobs, stock children, emoji icons, fake social proof, awards, user numbers, or testimonials.
- Use custom SVG checks, paper, pencil, print, and arrow icons.

- Keep H1 left aligned and maximum 3 lines. Avoid a generic centered hero.
- Preserve exact text in HTML; do not hide SEO copy in images.

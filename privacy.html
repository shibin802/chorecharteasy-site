Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

ChoreChartEasy is intended for parents and caregivers. Children should not create accounts or submit personal information.

Information we collect

The free maker works without an account. Google sign-in gives us your verified email, display name, profile image, and Google account identifier so we can operate your account and membership.

Chart data

Your current chart draft and, for Pro members, up to 20 saved plans—including any child names, ages, chores, and completion checks—are stored locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. These plans do not sync across devices. Avoid entering a child’s full name, address, school, health information, or other sensitive data, especially on a shared device.

Payments and providers

Creem processes checkout, tax, receipts, refunds, and subscription billing as merchant of record. We store transaction and subscription identifiers, not full card details. We use Google for authentication and Cloudflare for hosting and database services.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, traffic sources, and how visitors interact with the service. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and process technical information such as device, browser, approximate location, and visited pages. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and payment records as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Request account deletion by email. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove your local draft.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information.

Questions or data requests: support@chorecharteasy.com.

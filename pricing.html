BEFORE YOU UPGRADE

A few good questions.

Small plan. Clear terms.

What changes when I upgrade?

Plus removes the ChoreChartEasy watermark from prints and PDFs made with the chart maker and chore randomizer while your plan is active. Editing and age-based starters are already free.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Open your account and choose Manage subscription to cancel renewal. You keep watermark-free printing until your current paid period ends. After that, you can continue using the free maker with watermarked prints.

Will my charts be saved to my account?

No. Your chart stays in this browser, which keeps one active draft. Plus does not add cloud storage or device syncing. Print or save your current chart as a PDF before making another.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes, for Plus. Google sign-in connects your subscription to your account. You can use the free maker without signing in.

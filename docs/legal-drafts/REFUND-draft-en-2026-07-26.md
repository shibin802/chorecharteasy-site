### 4. Refund Requests

Subject to applicable law, we review refund requests submitted within 14 days of the initial purchase for issues including:

- duplicate charges;
- inability to access the files after reasonable troubleshooting;
- corrupted or materially incomplete files;
- delivered content that is materially different from the product description;
- other rights required by applicable law.

Where permitted by law, we generally do not provide refunds solely for a change of mind after the digital files have been successfully accessed or downloaded. This does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

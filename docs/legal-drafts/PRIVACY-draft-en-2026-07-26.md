## 1. Information You Provide

### Chart content

The chart maker lets you enter a nickname or initials, an age selection, chart titles, chores, and completion marks. The validation version is designed to store this content locally in your browser. We do not intentionally send chart content to our servers or analytics providers.

Please do not enter a child’s full name, address, school, health information, precise location, or other sensitive information. Local browser data may be visible to other people who use the same device or browser profile.

### Early-access email

If you join an early-access list, we collect your email address to send information about the product you selected. You can unsubscribe at any time. We do not require marketing consent to use the free chart maker.

### Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include. Do not send full payment-card information, passwords, children’s sensitive information, or authentication codes by email.

### Purchases

When live purchases become available, Creem will process checkout as Merchant of Record. Creem may collect payment, billing, tax, and transaction information under its own privacy policy. We expect to receive limited order information such as customer email, product code, order identifier, payment status, refund/dispute status, and timestamps. We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

No live purchase is currently available unless the checkout page clearly states that it is in live mode.

## 2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Service, our hosting and security provider may process IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps, and network/security logs to deliver and protect the Service.

With your permission, we may use analytics to understand page visits and product usage. Analytics is off until you choose it. Analytics events must not include names, exact ages, chart titles, chore text, completion details, email addresses, account identifiers, or order identifiers.

## 3. Local Storage and Cookies

We use local browser storage to keep one active chart draft and your preferences on the current device. You can remove this information with the Service’s “Clear local data” control or through your browser settings.

We use necessary storage to operate the Service. We use analytics cookies only after consent. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) and use “Cookie settings” in the footer to change your choice.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Service;
- remember your local chart and settings on your device;
- respond to support and privacy requests;
- send requested early-access messages;
- measure product usage after analytics consent;
- process and support purchases when live payment is enabled;
- prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use chart content to build advertising profiles. We do not use children’s chart content to train generative AI models.

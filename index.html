Frequently asked questions

Is ChoreChartEasy free?

Yes. The starter templates, editing, and printing are free. Free prints include a light background watermark.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the free chart maker. Google sign-in is required only to purchase Pro and keep membership linked to you.

What ages are supported?

The family generator supports ages 3–12, with simpler visible tasks for younger children and gradually more independent responsibilities for older kids. Always adapt suggestions to your child’s abilities and safety needs.

Can I make one fair chart for siblings?

Yes. Add up to four children with different ages. Each child receives age-appropriate personal jobs, plus shared family responsibilities that can be rotated instead of forcing every child to do identical work.

Where is my chart saved?

Your current draft is stored locally in this browser. It is not uploaded to our server. Clearing browser storage removes the draft.

What does Pro Family add?

Pro Family adds age-aware weekly rotations, a local library for up to 20 reusable plans, premium family and reward templates, four print styles, and watermark-free printing. The plan library stays on this device; cross-device cloud sync is not currently included.

How does Pro payment work?

Creem processes checkout. Membership is granted only after our server receives and verifies Creem’s signed payment webhook. The current checkout is test mode and does not make a real charge.

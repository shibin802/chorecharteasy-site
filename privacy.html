Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Policy explains how the website known as ChoreChartEasy (“ChoreChartEasy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when adults use https://chorecharteasy.com.

1. Scope

The current Service is a free browser-based chore chart maker. It does not require an account, does not accept live payments, and does not collect early-access email addresses through the website.

ChoreChartEasy is for parents, caregivers, teachers, and other adults. It is not intended for children to use independently.

2. Local chart data

The chart maker lets you enter a nickname or initials, an age group, a chart title, task text, and completion marks. The current Service processes this chart content in your browser and stores one active draft in local storage on that device. It does not send chart content to our application servers or analytics events.

Avoid full names, addresses, school names, health information, precise locations, or other sensitive information. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see local data.

3. Data processed automatically

Cloudflare provides hosting, content delivery, network security, and same-origin server functions. It may process IP address, browser and device information, requested URLs, timestamps, and security or network data needed to deliver and protect the Service.

4. Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is off until you choose “Accept analytics.” If accepted, it may process browser identifiers, page paths, device information, approximate region, and allowlisted product events.

Analytics events do not include names, exact ages, chart titles, task text, completion details, email addresses, account identifiers, or order identifiers. Google Signals, advertising storage, ad personalization, and cross-context behavioral advertising are not enabled by this consent flow.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, authentication codes, or children’s personal information.

6. Service providers

  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery, security, network processing, Pages Functions, and related infrastructure.
  • Google Analytics 4 — optional, consent-based website and product analytics.

These providers process information under their own policies. We will update this Policy before enabling another provider that materially changes the current data flow.

7. Retention

  • Active chart draft: stays in your browser until you use “Clear local data,” clear site data, or the browser removes it.
  • Consent choice: stays in local storage for up to 180 days, then the Service asks again.
  • Analytics identifiers: follow Google’s configured expiration unless you withdraw consent or clear browser data. Withdrawing through Cookie settings stops future analytics and attempts to remove first-party _ga cookies available to the site.
  • Cloudflare network and security data: retained under Cloudflare’s service configuration and policies. We do not maintain a separate copy of chart content in those logs.
  • Support messages: kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, protect the Service, or meet applicable obligations.

8. Your choices

  • Use the free maker without analytics.
  • Reject or withdraw analytics through Cookie settings.
  • Use “Clear local data” or browser controls to remove the active chart draft.
  • Email us to ask about personal information in a support conversation.

Where supported, Global Privacy Control keeps non-essential analytics disabled. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional access, correction, deletion, objection, or appeal rights.

9. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. Adults should supervise use, use nicknames or initials instead of full names, and keep sensitive information out of charts and support messages. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

10. Security

We use technical and administrative measures appropriate to the current Service, but no website, browser storage, email system, or transmission method is completely secure. Consider shared-device access before saving a chart.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy when the Service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and request a new consent choice when appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@chorecharteasy.com. ChoreChartEasy is the name used for this website.
